ViralCut AI Knowledge Base

Everything you need to know about viral retention algorithms, hook detection, and clip generation.

How does the AI calculate Viral Potential Scores?
ViralCut AI evaluates segments across 6 core retention vectors: 1. Opening Hook Strength (first 3-5 seconds curiosity or shock gap), 2. Emotional Sentiment Variance (acoustic shifts, surprise, contrarian theses), 3. Speech Pacing (140-180 words per minute sweet spot), 4. Narrative Completeness (self-contained point), 5. Information Density, and 6. Shareability Ratio. Scores range between 0 and 100. Clips are always ranked automatically with the highest score appearing FIRST.
What are the 8 stages of video analysis?
When you click "Analyze Video", the engine runs through:
  1. Extracting video information: Inspects codec, resolution, and frame rates.
  2. Extracting audio: Demuxes clean audio track and normalizes volume.
  3. Transcribing speech: Deep acoustic speech-to-text with word timestamps.
  4. Analyzing scenes & conversations: Identifies visual cuts and speaker clusters.
  5. Identifying engaging moments: Detects punchlines, hooks, and takeaways.
  6. Calculating viral potential: Multi-factor virality prediction.
  7. Generating clips: 9:16 vertical smart crop and animated caption synthesis.
  8. Ranking clips: Highest potential ranked at #1.
How does backend integration work?
ViralCut AI frontend communicates via standard REST endpoints: POST /api/video/analyze, POST /api/video/upload, GET /api/video/status/:jobId, and GET /api/video/clips/:jobId. In standalone browser mode, it operates an authentic deterministic AI simulation. You can connect your custom Python Flask or Node.js server in the Settings tab.
